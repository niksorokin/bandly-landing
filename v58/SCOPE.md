# V58 scope

V58 is an isolated copy of V57. The only change is monthly money, matched to the client offer. V1–V57 and the live site stay as they were.

Offer bands, per month, excluding 5% VAT:

- Up to 30 transactions: AED 690
- 31–90: AED 990
- 91–180: AED 1,490
- 181–280: AED 1,990
- 280+: Custom

The FAQ no longer says AED 399 to 3,000+. Inclusions, add-on names, and the rest of the page are unchanged.
