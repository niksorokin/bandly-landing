Clear answers

The questions worth asking first.

Is Bandly software or an accounting firm?

Bandly is an accounting firm. Automation helps collect, sort and prepare the work; professionals review important outputs and remain accountable for judgement.

Do I need to replace the systems I already use?

No. The starting point is the way your records already arrive: email, WhatsApp, shared drives and existing accounting systems.

What happens if a document is missing?

The gap stays visible. You receive one precise request for what is needed rather than a chain of generic reminders.

Will I be pushed straight into a sales call?

No. The first step is a short intake. A call comes later only if it helps clarify your company’s handover and scope.
